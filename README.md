**Welcome Pioneer, to the Platform Expansion Program.**

The **Platform Expansion Program** replaces natural resource deposits and exploration with a tightly focused, efficiency-driven challenge. Instead of roaming for nodes, you'll build and place **deployable resource nodes**, expanding your platform piece by piece to maximize output under extreme resource constraints.

**Your goal:**
Expand, optimize, and push your factory to the limits while balancing limited space and escalating demands. Every phase unlocks new technologies to grow your platform and automate production, culminating in a fully self-sustaining factory suspended in the sky.

## **What's New in 1.1 — The Science Rework**

The old purity-based science capsules are gone, and all PEP research has been removed from the HUB/MaM trees. Progression is now driven by a dedicated **science research system**:

- **Four science disciplines** — *Chemical*, *Electrical*, *Manufacturing*, and *Material* science, each with its own theme and production chain.
- **Science Kiosk** — a new building where you set and manage active research tasks.
- **Science Processors** — one dedicated processor per discipline. Feed it valid science to earn progress toward alternate recipe unlocks.
- **The science pipeline** — craft **Loose Science**, seal it into its **Science Container**, and **process** it into finished science.
- **Space Elevator integration** — cooked science is now a phase completion requirement, not just an optional side track.
- **Still sinks** — surplus science can be submitted to the AWESOME Sink for extra coupons.

## **Core Features**
- **Buildable Resource Nodes:**
  Placeable nodes for every resource type — upgradeable in place.
- **Extreme Resource Constraints:**
  Begin with minimal resources and slowly scale through careful planning.
- **Science-Driven Progression:**
  Research alternate recipes by completing science tasks at the Science Kiosk.
- **Integrated Liquids System:**
  Water and oil are generated through alternate production methods.
- **Live Factory Map:**
  The in-game map now renders a real-time top-down view of your factory, with one-click export.
- **No Soft-Lock Starts:**
  Starting a Platform Expansion Program game skips the onboarding sequence entirely.
- **No Dependencies:**
  This is a standalone experience—no other mods required.
